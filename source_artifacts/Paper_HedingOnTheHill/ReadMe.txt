/*# The program for "Hedging on the Hill: Does political hedging reduce firm risk?" by Christensen, Jin, Sridharan, and Wellman#*/

estimate.do is the Stata code for tables in the paper and in the Online Appendix.
firmcyclepanel.dta is a panel at the firm-election cycle level.
firmdaypanel.dta is a panel at the firm-day level.

Note that the datasets have already been winsorized at the top and bottom 1% levels. 

All variable definitions and data sources are listed in Appendix A of the paper.
